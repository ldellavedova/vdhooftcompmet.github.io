Liese is pursuing an MSc in Bioinformatics and Systems Biology, following a BSc in Biotechnology. For her master’s thesis, she is aligning the graph from PlantMetWiki with published plant metabolomics data under the supervision of Justin van der Hooft, Elena Del Pup, and Larissa Della Vedova. By connecting the existing data with the knowledge graph, her project aims to extend the graph with new metabolites.
