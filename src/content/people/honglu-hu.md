Honglu obtained her BSc in Horticulture from Jilin University and her MSc in Pomology from Zhejiang University. During her master's research, she investigated polymethoxyflavone biosynthesis in citrus, focusing on identifying key O-methyltransferases and suitable precursors, while also contributing to research on its transcriptional regulation. This experience deepened her interest in plant specialized metabolism and metabolic diversity.
Having previously focused mainly on experimental work, Honglu is keen to expand her bioinformatics skills. In 2026, she started her sandwich PhD under the supervision of Prof. Marnix Medema and Prof. Justin van der Hooft at Wageningen University & Research, and Prof. Li Wang at the Agricultural Genomics Institute at Shenzhen, Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Her PhD research focuses on understanding specialized metabolism in Apiaceae, from exploring chemical diversity to reconstructing biosynthetic pathways, with the broader aim of discovering novel bioactive metabolites from medicinal plants for potential applications in drug discovery.

Research interests: Plant specialized metabolism, omics, and synthetic biology.

Hobbies: Hiking, cycling, badminton, traveling, drawing, singing, and exploring new foods.
