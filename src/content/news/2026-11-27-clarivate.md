Thanks to my fantastic CompMet team for their great, fantastic, crazy, and constructive ideas and the collaborative spirit ⭐ 🎉 🐐

This is the first time that our work appears in the Clarivate list, in the cross-omics field. What this tells me, is that the work we are doing is recognized internationally and that groups worldwide, both in academia and industry, are applying our omics tools for biochemical and biological discoveries, and that several groups are also co-developing the tools with us.

I think this is a fantastic achievement and whilst it is my name that appears in the list - I owe this to my CompMet team! 🙌

In order of appearance, let me thank some by name for their mentorship, guidance, and inspiration - you all made or are making a lasting impact on me! 😊 
Jacques Vervoort, Ric De Vos, Mike Barret, Fidele Tugizimana, Simon Rogers, Marnix Medema, and Pieter Dorrestein.

Also thanks to all past members from BSc and MSc students to PhDs and PostDocs! You made the group into what it is now, and your legacy lives on 😎

And yes, I realize how this award is based on citations - a much debated way to show impact of science. Yet, I think that by "standing on giants" and acknowledging ideas and the tools and resources that shape and enable current work, the citation network does can tell a story of how ideas and resources are reused....if we create the reference list with due diligence that is....

#ProudPI in #CompMetabolomics & #MultiOMics #HighlyCited2026
